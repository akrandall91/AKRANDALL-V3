B2G support from foundation to follow-through

You do not need to arrive with a finished government-sales system.

AKRD can help build the practical pieces between deciding to pursue public work and becoming easier for an agency, prime, or partner to understand, evaluate, and engage.

01 / REGISTER

Set up the right accounts

Guided SAM.gov entity-registration and renewal support, state and local vendor portals, profile preparation, and a clear record of what must stay current.

02 / POSITION

Define where you fit

Clarify the offer, target agencies, prime and partner opportunities, contract pathways, decision-makers, and the business case for choosing you.

03 / PRESENT

Create buyer-ready material

Build capability statements, service sheets, presentations, case stories, proposal content, and proof libraries that make the business easier to evaluate.

04 / FIND

Choose the right events and rooms

Identify and qualify trade shows, industry conferences, matchmaking events, and agency outreach opportunities based on market fit—not attendance volume.

05 / PROPOSE

Coordinate a stronger response

Use bid/no-bid discipline, compliance matrices, writing support, reusable content, contributor coordination, and review checkpoints to improve the proposal process.

06 / OPERATE

Keep the market work connected

Build opportunity trackers, follow-up workflows, calendars, document systems, and post-award handoffs so progress does not live in one inbox or one person’s memory.

You stay in control of official representations. AKRD can guide account setup, prepare information, and coordinate the process; your business owns each account and approves or signs its registrations, certifications, pricing, and legal attestations.
