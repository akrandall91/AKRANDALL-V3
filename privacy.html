PRIVACY

Privacy in plain language

You stay in control of the information you share.

Here is what the website collects when you send a message, complete the free assessment, or book an intro call, why it is used, and how to request its removal.

01 / WHAT YOU SHARE

Messages and assessment answers

The contact form collects the details you enter. The free assessment collects your answers, contact details, and tailored recommendation. The site may also note the page you came from and how you found it so your request can be understood and answered.

02 / STORAGE

Where your information is kept

When direct delivery is active, messages and completed assessments are stored in a private Google Sheet accessible to AK Randall Digital. Your information is not sold or used to build unrelated marketing lists.

03 / BOOKING

When you book through Google Calendar

Google provides the embedded booking calendar. The information you enter there is handled under Google's terms and is used to create, confirm, and manage your appointment.

04 / ANALYTICS

How site performance is measured

The site may record page views, general traffic source, device category, scroll depth, and actions such as starting an assessment or requesting a call. Analytics can include broad outcome labels, such as an assessment path or recommendation, but never a name, email address, phone number, company name, message, or detailed response summary.

05 / REPORTING

Private notifications and summaries

Lead notifications and aggregate site-performance summaries may be emailed to AK Randall Digital so inquiries can be answered and the website can be improved. These reports are for internal business use.

06 / YOUR CHOICES

Ask, correct, or remove

To ask what information is held, correct your message, or request deletion, email andrew@akrandall.com.
